Pedro Correia is a game producer, researcher and technology entrepreneur. His work brings together digital games, artificial intelligence, communication and socio-environmental impact to create experiences and tools with public relevance.

He holds a Master's degree in Creative Media and a Bachelor's degree in International Relations from UFRJ. His research explores digital games as tools for communication, mobilization and environmental regeneration, including *Tree Tales* and the concept of regenerative digital games.

At OASIS, he coordinates technology experimentation, connecting research and development in projects focused on inclusion, social participation and access to information.
