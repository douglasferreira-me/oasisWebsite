Tomaz Cesário Alvim Martinelli is a lawyer, entrepreneur and researcher with an interdisciplinary practice. In the legal field, he supports people, companies and organizations in the game industry's creative ecosystem on corporate law, fundraising, data protection, contracts, game publishing and intellectual property.

As an entrepreneur, he takes part in projects related to audiovisual production and sustainability. His research focuses on internet regulation and emerging technologies, bringing together legal, cultural and economic questions.

At OASIS, he coordinates Thematic Studies, contributing to the investigation of public debates and challenges related to digital technologies.
