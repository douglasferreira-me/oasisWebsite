## More accessible legislative research

BuscaPL makes it easier to explore bills under consideration in the Brazilian Chamber of Deputies and Federal Senate. The platform brings together text search, filters and statistical visualizations to support the discovery of bills and the reading of trends in legislative debate.

An artificial intelligence model developed for legislative texts supports thematic search and filtering. The tool helps researchers, journalists, organizations and the public find relevant bills and follow the legislative process with more context.
